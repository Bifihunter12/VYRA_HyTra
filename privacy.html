Privacy

Last updated · 3 Oct 2026

What VYRA stores

Your workout settings (durations, treadmill speed, cue preferences) and any workouts you choose to save are kept only in your browser's localStorage. Nothing is sent to a server. There are no accounts, cookies, analytics or tracking.

Audio, speech and vibration

Beeps, spoken cues and vibration are generated on your device. Speech uses your browser's built-in voice; no audio is recorded.

Deleting your data

Delete saved workouts from the History screen, or clear your browser's site data to remove everything.
