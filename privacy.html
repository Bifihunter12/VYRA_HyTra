Health & safety

VYRA is a workout guide. It is not medical advice, physical therapy, rehabilitation or a diagnosis tool, and it does not replace a doctor, physiotherapist or coach.

Check with a doctor before starting if you are pregnant, have a heart, lung or joint condition, an injury, or haven't exercised in a while. Scale weights, speeds and rounds to what you can do with good form.

Stop immediately if you feel chest pain, dizziness, shortness of breath beyond normal effort, or sharp or worsening pain. The “pain or injury” check-in only suggests rest and gentle mobility; it does not assess injuries.

What VYRA stores

Your profile (weekly goal, level, equipment), workout settings, program progress and your workout history are kept only in your browser's localStorage. Nothing is sent to a server. There are no accounts, cookies, analytics or tracking.

Audio, speech and vibration

Beeps, spoken cues and vibration are switched off in this version. When they return, they will be generated on your device using your browser's built-in voice; no audio is ever recorded.

Deleting your data

Delete saved workouts from the History screen, or clear your browser's site data to remove everything.
