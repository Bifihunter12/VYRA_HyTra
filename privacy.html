Health & safety

VYRA is a workout guide. It is not medical advice, physical therapy, rehabilitation or a diagnosis tool, and it does not replace a doctor, physiotherapist or coach.

Check with a doctor before starting if you are pregnant, have a heart, lung or joint condition, an injury, or haven't exercised in a while. Scale weights, speeds and rounds to what you can do with good form.

Stop immediately if you feel chest pain, dizziness, shortness of breath beyond normal effort, or sharp or worsening pain. The “pain or injury” check-in only suggests rest and gentle mobility; it does not assess injuries.

What VYRA stores

Your profile (weekly goal, level, equipment), workout settings, program progress and your workout history are kept in your browser's localStorage. Without an account, nothing is sent to a server. There are no cookies, analytics or tracking.

Optional account & cloud sync

If you sign in (with your email or Google), VYRA stores your email address, your workout history (times, distances, ratings and how it felt) and your settings and plan progress so they can sync across your devices. Sample data is never uploaded.

This data is stored with Supabase, on servers in the United States. Each account can only read its own data. When you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and name from Google; we don't get your password.

Athlete profile & leaderboards

Your athlete profile is private and off the leaderboards until you change that in Profile → Athlete profile. If you join the leaderboards, other athletes see your display name, your best score for each benchmark and your rank. Never your email, your other workouts or your history. A public profile also shows your handle and division. Results marked “Training” are never shown to anyone.

Following, feed & comments

Sharing your activity is off until you turn it on. With “Followers”, athletes who follow you see your workouts and results in their feed; with “Everyone”, anyone who opens your public profile can. Only athletes with a public profile can be followed. Reactions and comments are only possible on activity you are allowed to see, and you can delete comments on your own posts. Results saved as “Training” never show a score.

You can sign out at any time (your workouts stay on the device), or delete your account in Profile → Account & sync, which permanently removes your cloud copy and login.

Monthly challenge results

If you're on the leaderboards, your display name, score and final place in each monthly challenge are kept as that month's result, so everyone can see who won. Deleting your account removes them.

Events & video verification

Registering for an event shares your display name, division and event results in that event's standings. To verify a result you can send a link to a video you host yourself (for example on YouTube); VYRA stores only the link and your note, and only VYRA review staff can see them.

Clubs & location

Your city and country are optional and only used for local leaderboards. Club members see each other's display names in the roster; invite codes are visible to the club's owner and admins only. If you delete your account you leave all clubs; clubs you created stay for their other members.

Voice coach, beeps and vibration

The coach's voice is a set of recordings that come with the app (made with an open-source voice model, no real person's voice). They play on your device; nothing you say or do is recorded or sent anywhere. If a line has no recording yet, your device's built-in voice reads it instead.

Deleting your data

Delete single workouts from History, erase everything on a device in Profile → Data, and delete your account (and its cloud copy) in Profile → Account & sync.
